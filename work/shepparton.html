Shepparton

I am Ashley Garner. I am in Shepparton, in the Goulburn Valley. 3630 is the postcode and the album.

The town is hectic in the ordinary way. Maude Street early. Mooroopna across the bridge. Fruit trucks. A week that does not finish when the clock says it should.

I write that as pressure and home. I do not write a crime map. I do not name a neighbour. A song is not a report.

If you searched Shepparton or the Goulburn Valley and landed here, you found my site. Not a newsroom.
