Vision

This page is a vision, not a result.

A finished 3630 album from Shepparton. Official videos staggered under Ash Garner. A merch line that only uses the marks I own. A desk that can brief a track in one sentence and check it: no fake plays, no fake degrees, no crime map, no child in the art.

Gold in the vision means the work gets finished and signed. It does not mean a chart or a news story I have not earned.

If I rise, I do not rise over Shepp. I rise out of it and bring the name back.
