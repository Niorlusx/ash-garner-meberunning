ARTIST

Ash Garner

Trap artist from Shepparton, Victoria — postcode 3630.

His music blends raw regional storytelling with dark, heavy production and the energy of Melbourne streets. Under the Meberunning banner he releases unfiltered tracks that speak from the ground up: loyalty, pressure, local life, and the grind of regional Australia.

Known for authentic lyricism and a no-blueprint approach, Ash Garner is building a catalog that stays true to the 3630 while reaching beyond it.

Shepparton to the world. 3630 forever.
